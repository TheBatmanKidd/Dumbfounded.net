About Antonio Vivaldi

Antonio Vivaldi (full name Antonio Lucio Vivaldi) was a famous Italian composer born on March 4, 1678 in Venice, Italy and died on July 28, 1741. Some famous pieces include:

  • The Four Seasons
  • L'estro armonico
  • Gloria in D major
  • L'Olimpiade
  • Stabat Mater
Antonio had composed many pieces for the violin, and more instruments. Antonio was He was ordained a Catholic priest in Venice in March 1703 at the age of 25, but had to stop doing masses because of health problems.
